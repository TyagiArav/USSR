The Platform

Five decrees for the people

Signed, stamped, and laminated in the student union. Our positions on the issues that keep you up at night, aside from the 8 a.m. class.

1 Environment & Conservation

Plant the trees. Then collectivize them.

Every student plants one tree. The trees belong to everyone, so nobody may carve initials into them. Solar panels go on every roof, including the roof of the parking office, which is the only thing that office will produce.

Recycling becomes mandatory. Including old jokes. Especially this one.

2 Inflation & Economy

One meal swipe, one potato.

We peg the dining-hall swipe to the potato, the most stable currency known to humankind. Textbook prices are frozen at their 1987 level. The campus bookstore will be renamed the People's Bookstore and will finally have the edition your professor asked for.

Economists consulted: one. He was a sophomore. He agreed.

3 Campus Safety

A campus where the only shots are flu shots.

Real safety first: more counselors, working blue-light phones, lit walkways, and a safe-ride van that shows up in under four hours. The only arms race on campus will be the annual arm-wrestling tournament.

Water balloons remain legal during Spring Fest, in designated zones.

4 People's Rights & Full Equality

Everyone equal. Everyone gets the same dorm room.

Equal rights for every student regardless of race, gender, religion, or who they are. Everyone is treated with dignity. Also, every dorm room is now identical. Yes, it is the one with the radiator that clanks at 3 a.m. Fairness has a price.

Seniors lose priority registration. Freshmen lose nothing, because they had nothing.

5 Frat Parties

There are no more parties. There is only the Party.

We support fun, done safely. All Greek life is merged into one fraternity, Alpha Sigma Sigma, which hosts one enormous, well-lit, respectful event every Friday. Sober monitors, free water, and a playlist voted on by the people.

The DJ is elected. The DJ can be recalled.
